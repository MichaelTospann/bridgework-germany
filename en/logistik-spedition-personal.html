Legal Basis

What you should know as an employer

The legal pathway differs significantly depending on the position – we advise honestly on what's realistic.

EU/EFTA Nationals

Freedom of movement applies to professionals from EU and EFTA states. No visa, no work permit required – including for drivers with an EU license.

Warehouse Staff from Third Countries

For warehouse helpers and order pickers from non-EU countries, we assess the appropriate residence and employment route individually. A recognition partnership under section 16d(3) of the Residence Act is not a general route for helper jobs. It requires an eligible qualification, at least A2 German, a suitable employer and normally qualified employment. Ordinary helper work is insufficient.

To be honest: truck drivers are more complex

§24a BeschV for drivers from third countries

A separate regulation (§24a BeschV) applies to truck drivers from third countries. It allows qualification in Germany – linked to a 15-month deadline, driving license conversion, and language requirements for theory and safety exams.

This is feasible, but more time-consuming than warehouse positions. We tell you transparently whether the effort is worthwhile for your business – instead of making false promises.

FAQ

What employers ask us most often

Can you also place truck drivers?

Yes, although the legal pathway is more complex than for warehouse staff. Under §24a BeschV, qualification in Germany within 15 months is often required. We advise transparently on whether this pathway makes sense for your business.

What does the placement cost?

The placement fee is 1.5 times the fixed gross monthly salary, with a minimum of EUR 2,480 net plus VAT per placement. Services, payment terms and any replacement arrangements are agreed in writing. Additional costs are planned transparently outside the agency fee. Candidates pay no recruitment fees.

How quickly can a warehouse helper position be filled?

A warehouse role is not automatically a fast immigration route. Candidate availability, documentation and the required employment and residence procedures determine the timetable. We discuss the individual start window without promising a fixed visa or employment start date.

Do candidates need a forklift license?

A valid certificate is required for forklift operator positions. We check in advance whether the foreign license can be recognised or whether retraining in Germany makes sense.

Do you also help with accommodation?

Where possible, we coordinate an accommodation option with you. Availability, terms and additional costs are clarified in advance; accommodation or a particular length of employment is not guaranteed.
