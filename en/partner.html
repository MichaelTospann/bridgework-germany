A useful introduction

BridgeWork Germany brings international applicants and businesses together through direct placement. We start with a concrete role and agreed requirements. Applicants pay no recruitment fees. We are also available to prepare existing candidate files and organise a structured intake.

You can forward this page to a business with a current need. The company can describe its requirements directly. A partner agreement and payment terms are settled separately before any profiles are shared.
