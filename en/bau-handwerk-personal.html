Legal Basis

What you should know as an employer

For construction and trade professions, there are several legally compliant routes to international recruitment.

EU/EFTA Nationals

Freedom of movement applies to professionals from EU and EFTA states. No visa, no work permit required.

Professionals from Third Countries

For construction helpers and other support roles filled by applicants from non-EU countries, an appropriate residence and employment route must be assessed individually. Section 16d(3) of the Residence Act is not a general route for helper jobs. A recognition partnership requires an eligible qualification, at least A2 German, a suitable employer and normally qualified employment. Ordinary helper work is insufficient.

Especially relevant for construction & trades

The Western Balkans Regulation

With a concrete job offer, nationals from six Western Balkan countries can enter Germany – without formal recognition of professional qualifications in non-regulated professions.

The statutory quota allows up to 50,000 Federal Employment Agency approvals per calendar year, not 50,000 visas. It is allocated by month and nationality. Availability, official approvals, the residence permit and processing times must be checked individually; fast entry is not guaranteed.

Source: §26 Employment Ordinance (BeschV), Federal Employment Agency

FAQ

What employers ask us most often

What exactly is the Western Balkans regulation?

The Western Balkans Regulation can provide an employment route for nationals of Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo, Montenegro, North Macedonia and Serbia. Recognition of a professional qualification is generally not required for non-regulated occupations. A specific job offer, the necessary approvals and the appropriate residence permit are still required. The quota allows up to 50,000 Federal Employment Agency approvals per calendar year, allocated by month and nationality; it is not a visa or timing guarantee.

What does the placement cost?

The placement fee is 1.5 times the fixed gross monthly salary, with a minimum of EUR 2,480 net plus VAT per placement. Services, payment terms and any replacement arrangements are agreed in writing. Additional costs are planned transparently outside the agency fee. Candidates pay no recruitment fees.

Do tradespeople need formal recognition?

It depends on the profession. For many helper and semi-skilled roles, no formal recognition is needed. For regulated professions or where recognition is desired, we fully support the procedure.

How quickly can a position be filled?

The start depends on candidate availability, documentation, the appropriate employment route, quotas and official appointments. We agree a realistic timetable. The Western Balkans Regulation does not guarantee fast entry or a particular employment start date.

Do you also help with accommodation?

Where possible, we coordinate an accommodation option with you. Availability, terms and additional costs are clarified in advance; accommodation or a particular length of employment is not guaranteed.
