Staffing for your business

A clear staffing enquiry for direct placement in Germany.

Your requirement. A clear next step.

Tell us the role, location, working hours, pay and qualifications. You can enquire across industries. We review the concrete conditions and coordinate useful next steps.

Describe your staffing need ↗

The form is available in German and English. No Google account is required. Your enquiry is nonbinding.

Direct employment

Your company and the employee sign their employment contract directly. We coordinate requirements, profiles and the agreed preparation.

A transparent fee

1.5 fixed monthly gross salaries, at least EUR 2,480 per placement, plus VAT. Services and payment terms are agreed in the specific placement mandate.

Fair recruitment

Applicants pay no recruitment fee. We follow the ILO principles on fair recruitment. Personal profiles are shared only after a separate release for the named employer.
