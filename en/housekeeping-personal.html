Legal Basis

What you should know as an employer

International recruitment has become significantly easier since the Skilled Immigration Act – we know the details.

EU/EFTA Nationals

Freedom of movement applies to professionals from EU and EFTA states. No visa, no work permit required – hiring is just as straightforward as for German applicants.

Professionals from Third Countries

For housekeeping support roles filled by applicants from non-EU countries, we assess the appropriate residence and employment route individually. A recognition partnership under section 16d(3) of the Residence Act is not a general route for helper jobs. It requires an eligible qualification, at least A2 German, a suitable employer and normally qualified employment. Ordinary helper work is insufficient.

Skilled Immigration Act

Since March 2020, the opportunities for recruiting skilled workers from abroad have been significantly expanded. For hospitality employers, this means more legal, plannable ways to hire international staff.

We handle the complete coordination – from application to recognition of professional qualifications, where required.

FAQ

What employers ask us most often

Do applicants need German language skills?

We agree the required language level with the employer according to the role, workplace communication and employment route. We assess language skills before introducing candidates.

What does the placement cost?

The placement fee is 1.5 times the fixed gross monthly salary, with a minimum of EUR 2,480 net plus VAT per placement. Services, payment terms and any replacement arrangements are agreed in writing. Additional costs are planned transparently outside the agency fee. Candidates pay no recruitment fees.

How long does the process take?

The timetable depends on candidate availability, documentation, the appropriate employment route and official procedures. We discuss a realistic start window; we cannot guarantee a fixed visa or employment start date.

What happens if it's not a good fit?

We first clarify the specific issue and work towards a solution together. Any replacement arrangement and its scope are set out in the recruitment contract; we do not offer an unlimited replacement guarantee.

Do you also help with accommodation?

Where possible, we coordinate an accommodation option with you. Availability, terms and additional costs are clarified in advance; accommodation or a particular length of employment is not guaranteed.
