What we provide

Included in the placement

  • Candidate selection according to your requirements
  • Visa and authority coordination
  • Support with accommodation search
  • Cultural onboarding of candidates
  • Welcome package & first steps in Germany
  • Aftercare following employment
  • Agreeing fair working conditions and coordinating contract documents

What you pay

Services, the fee trigger and payment terms are agreed in the recruitment contract

  • The initial consultation and requirements assessment are free and non-binding
  • Applicants never pay – costs are covered exclusively by the employer
  • Our placement is via a one-time flat fee per successful placement
  • Placement fee: 1.5 times the fixed gross monthly salary, with a minimum of EUR 2,480 net plus VAT per placement

Our Approach

Ethical Recruitment.
Guided by ILO principles.

✓ ILO Employer Pays Principle

We commit to fair recruitment guided by ILO principles: no recruitment fees for candidates, transparent contracts and fair wages. The employer covers recruitment fees and related costs. ILO: General principles and operational guidelines for fair recruitment (2019).

Principle
The employer pays.
Never the applicant.

The employer covers recruitment costs and the associated visa and travel costs. We plan these and any other agreed expenses transparently in advance; third-party costs are not automatically included in the agency fee.
