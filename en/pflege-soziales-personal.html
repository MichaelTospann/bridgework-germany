Our Approach

Ethical recruitment in care is not optional – it's mandatory

Especially in healthcare, the way recruitment is conducted has direct effects on care provision in the countries of origin.

WHO Code of Practice

Respecting the rules on international health-worker recruitment

For health and nursing occupations, the recruitment restrictions under section 38 of the Employment Ordinance and the principles of the WHO Code of Practice must be considered. Before a search begins, we check the applicable rules, the role concerned and the permitted recruitment route.

We do not begin recruitment that is legally prohibited. These restrictions are assessed separately from the Employer Pays Principle: the employer covers recruitment costs and the associated visa and travel costs.

Source: WHO Global Code of Practice on the International Recruitment of Health Personnel; §38 BeschV (German Employment Ordinance)

Legal Basis

What you should know as an employer

Care is subject to special legal regulation – we know the details and provide structured support.

EU/EEA Nationals

An automatic recognition procedure may apply to certain EU/EEA qualifications and qualifications from Switzerland. The specific training documents, professional authorisation and any other requirements must be clarified with the competent authority.

Accelerated Skilled Worker Procedure

Where the requirements are met, the employer may initiate the fast-track skilled-worker procedure under section 81a of the Residence Act with the worker's authorisation. The current official fee is EUR 411; further costs are planned separately. Individual deadlines depend on complete documentation and other requirements. There is no fixed overall timetable or guarantee of a visa, entry or employment start.

Recognition Partnership

A recognition partnership may allow a start before recognition is complete where the qualification, language and employer requirements are met. Permitted duties and supervision are assessed individually. Work as a qualified nurse is not automatically allowed before the required professional authorisation.

Funding for Qualification Costs

The Federal Employment Agency covers up to 100% of training course costs for qualifying international nurses under certain conditions.

FAQ

What employers ask us most often

What's the difference between a care assistant and a qualified nurse?

Qualified nursing is a regulated profession; foreign qualifications and professional authorisation must be checked. Nursing assistance is not automatically a route without training or recognition: section 22a of the Employment Ordinance requires a recognised German nursing-assistance qualification or official recognition of an equivalent foreign qualification. Other requirements and Federal Employment Agency approval must be considered. General housekeeping is a separate role.

Which countries can't be actively recruited from?

For health and nursing occupations, we assess the current recruitment restrictions under section 38 of the Employment Ordinance and the WHO principles. The role concerned, the applicable rules and the permitted recruitment route matter. We do not begin active recruitment that is legally prohibited.

What does the placement cost?

The placement fee is 1.5 times the fixed gross monthly salary, with a minimum of EUR 2,480 net plus VAT per placement. Services, payment terms and any replacement arrangements are agreed in writing. Additional costs are planned transparently outside the agency fee. Candidates pay no recruitment fees.

How long does recognition of a qualified nurse take?

Timing depends on qualifications, complete documentation, the recognition authority and other official procedures. The procedure under section 81a of the Residence Act does not create a fixed overall timetable or guarantee a visa. We clarify the schedule for the individual case.

Can the nurse already work during the recognition process?

This may be possible where the requirements of an appropriate employment and recognition route are met. Duties, qualifications, required supervision and official permissions are assessed specifically. Employment as a nursing assistant or qualified nurse is not guaranteed in advance.
